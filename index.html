How we build

Six rules every app is built under

They're written down because they're expensive to keep — and the moment they're negotiable they stop being principles.

Read the full story→
I

Local-first, private by default

Your data lives on your device. No required accounts, no third-party trackers, and features work offline unless they genuinely need the network.

II

Deterministic core, AI at the edges

Where our apps use AI, testable deterministic logic makes every decision that matters — AI narrates, parses and suggests, and always shows its work.

III

Test-first where it counts

Core logic ships as isolated, fully unit-tested Swift packages — the math is proven before it gets a user interface.

IV

Accessibility is a gate

VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, Reduce Motion and contrast settings are release requirements, not follow-ups.

V

No dark patterns

Honest paywalls, one-tap cancel, and we never paywall your own data. If a subscription isn't worth it, cancelling should be easy.

VI

Safety before cleverness

Health and home-safety topics get conservative, clearly-disclaimed guidance that defers to qualified professionals.
