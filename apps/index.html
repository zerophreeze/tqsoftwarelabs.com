FuelUpHealth & Fitness · iPhone · Apple Watch

Lunch logged in five seconds.

FuelUp is low-friction tracking for the four numbers that actually move: calories, protein, water and body weight. Open the app, tap two chips, and lunch is logged in under five seconds. Everything lives in a local database on your iPhone — there is no account, no analytics, and no server of ours to leak.

FilmCameraPhoto & Video · iPhone

Shoot like film. On your phone.

FilmCamera is a camera that commits. Pick a look, frame it, and shoot — grain, halation, color response and print borders are rendered live in a custom Metal pipeline, so the viewfinder is the photograph. It also shoots video, a 1999 camcorder tape treatment, an ultrawide cinema format, and instant-print photo strips.

HomeOpsProductivity · iPhone

A private brain for your home.

HomeOps is the digital binder your house never came with. Two minutes of setup produces a maintenance plan matched to your home's type, age, region and systems; from there it holds your appliances, manuals, receipts, warranties, projects and contractor history, reminds you before things are due, and answers questions using your home's own data.
